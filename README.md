Swiggy Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes a Swiggy food delivery dataset containing more than 197,000 records from multiple cities and restaurants across India. The objective is to uncover business insights related to restaurant performance, food categories, pricing trends, and customer ratings.

Dataset Information

- Records: 197,430+
- Cities: 28
- Restaurants: 993
- Categories: Multiple food categories
- Features: City, State, Restaurant Name, Category, Dish Name, Price, Rating, Rating Count, Order Date

Objectives

- Analyze restaurant performance
- Identify popular food categories
- Study pricing trends
- Evaluate customer ratings
- Compare city-wise food offerings
- Generate business insights through visualizations

Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Excel

Analysis Performed

Data Cleaning

- Removed duplicate records
- Checked missing values
- Converted date columns
- Created additional time-based features

Exploratory Data Analysis

- Top cities by food listings
- Top restaurants by menu size
- Most popular food categories
- Average price by city
- Average price by category
- Highest-rated restaurants
- Rating distribution
- Price distribution
- Monthly trends
- Correlation analysis

Visualizations

- Bar Charts
- Line Charts
- Histograms
- Scatter Plots

Key Insights

- Major cities contribute the highest number of food listings.
- A small group of restaurants dominates menu availability.
- Popular food categories account for a significant portion of listings.
- Pricing varies considerably across cities and categories.
- Most restaurants maintain high customer ratings.
- Customer ratings and rating counts provide useful indicators of restaurant popularity.

Conclusion

The project demonstrates the use of data analytics techniques to extract actionable business insights from food delivery data. The findings can help restaurants and food delivery platforms improve decision-making related to pricing, customer experience, and market strategy.
